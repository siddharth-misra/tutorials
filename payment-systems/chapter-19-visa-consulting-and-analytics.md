# 19: Visa Consulting and Analytics

Not every payment capability is a network rail or a software platform. In a large ecosystem, data analysis, optimization guidance, and industry benchmarking can also become strategic products that help issuers, merchants, and fintechs improve growth, risk, and conversion.

This chapter explains why consulting and analytics matter inside payments rather than treating them as side businesses. The useful lens is that better decisions around fraud, acceptance, marketing, and portfolio management can increase the value participants get from the broader network.

---

## 19.1 Why Advisory Matters in Payments

Many payment problems are multi-dimensional: product, risk, pricing, UX, operations, and compliance.

Advisory services help organizations make better decisions using network and market insights.

---

## 19.2 Typical Consulting Themes

- portfolio growth strategy,
- approval-rate optimization,
- fraud reduction,
- customer lifecycle improvements,
- cross-border expansion planning.

---

## 19.3 Analytics as Strategic Asset

Analytics value comes from:

- transaction trend visibility,
- benchmark comparisons,
- segmentation insights,
- experiment and outcome measurement.

Insight quality depends on data quality and interpretation discipline.

---

## 19.4 Common Engagement Outcomes

1. Better auth performance.
2. Reduced fraud losses and false positives.
3. Improved campaign targeting.
4. More efficient operating processes.

---

## 19.5 Common Mistakes

1. Treating analytics as dashboard-only exercise.
2. Weak action planning after insights.
3. No ownership for post-recommendation execution.

---

## 19.6 Glossary

- **Benchmarking:** Comparing performance against peers or standards.
- **Segmentation:** Grouping users/transactions for targeted strategy.
- **KPI:** Key Performance Indicator.

## 19.8 Recap

- Analytics and advisory are major value-added differentiators.
- Insights matter only when converted into operational change.
- Data governance and ownership are essential for results.
