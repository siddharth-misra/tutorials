# 30: Hiring, Performance, and People Management

Payment platforms depend on people systems as much as on software systems. Hiring quality, feedback loops, role clarity, and fair performance calibration all shape whether a team can handle regulated complexity and sustained delivery pressure.

This chapter explains how to build those people systems in a practical way. The emphasis is on repeatable management mechanisms that improve talent density, accountability, and growth without turning the organization into a process-heavy machine.

---

## 30.1 Hiring for Payments Engineering

Strong hiring balances:

- technical depth,
- reliability mindset,
- security awareness,
- collaboration quality.

---

## 30.2 Interview Loop Design

Typical loop includes:

1. coding/problem-solving,
2. system design,
3. behavioral/collaboration,
4. domain-risk awareness.

Structured rubrics reduce bias.

---

## 30.3 Performance Management

Healthy performance systems focus on:

- clear expectations,
- regular feedback,
- measurable outcomes,
- growth planning.

Surprises at review time indicate process failure.

---

## 30.4 Promotion and Calibration

Promotion decisions should be:

- evidence-based,
- role-level aligned,
- calibrated across managers,
- transparent in criteria.

---

## 30.5 Retention and Leadership Pipeline

Retention improves with:

- meaningful work,
- career progression clarity,
- mentorship programs,
- manageable operational load.

---

## 30.6 Common Mistakes

1. Hiring only for short-term delivery speed.
2. Inconsistent evaluation standards.
3. Promotion decisions without clear evidence.

---

## 30.7 Glossary

- **Calibration:** Cross-team alignment of performance ratings.
- **Rubric:** Structured scoring framework.
- **Succession planning:** Preparing future leaders for key roles.

## 30.9 Recap

- Strong people systems are strategic assets in regulated engineering.
- Structured hiring and feedback improve fairness and quality.
- Retention and succession planning sustain long-term execution.
