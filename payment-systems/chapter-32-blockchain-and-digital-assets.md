# 32: Blockchain and Digital Assets

Blockchain and digital assets generate strong opinions in payments, but the useful question is not whether the technology is exciting. It is whether a specific use case gains real advantages in settlement speed, programmability, transparency, or asset design that justify the extra complexity.

This chapter explains the topic from a payments-operator perspective. It separates stablecoins, crypto-linked card products, and tokenized asset experiments so you can judge where the technology fits and where conventional rails are still stronger.

---

## 32.1 Why This Topic Matters

Digital asset infrastructure is influencing how organizations think about settlement speed, programmability, and cross-border value transfer.

---

## 32.2 Stablecoin Settlement Concepts

Stablecoins are digital assets designed to track a reference value (often fiat currency).

Potential relevance:

- near-real-time transfer possibilities,
- programmable settlement logic,
- new treasury and liquidity models.

Operational and regulatory constraints remain significant.

---

## 32.3 Crypto-Linked Cards

Crypto-linked card experiences can allow spending via card rails while funding logic interacts with digital-asset holdings.

Design considerations:

- conversion mechanics,
- pricing transparency,
- compliance and disclosures.

---

## 32.4 Platform Implications

Processor/platform teams may need:

- new ledger abstractions,
- asset conversion workflows,
- custody/partner interfaces,
- expanded risk/compliance tooling.

---

## 32.5 Risk and Governance

Critical risk areas:

- AML/sanctions,
- custody and key management,
- regulatory uncertainty,
- operational resilience of partners.

---

## 32.6 Common Mistakes

1. Treating digital assets as purely technical integration.
2. Ignoring legal and compliance complexity.
3. Weak customer communication on volatility/fees.

---

## 32.7 Glossary

- **Stablecoin:** Digital asset designed to maintain stable value reference.
- **Custody:** Safekeeping and control of digital assets/keys.
- **On-chain:** Activity recorded on blockchain network.

## 32.9 Recap

- Digital asset use cases are expanding but operational complexity is high.
- Stablecoin and crypto-linked models require strong risk and compliance controls.
- Integration success depends on governance as much as technology.
