# 33: AI and Machine Learning

AI and machine learning create value in payments when they improve decisions or automate work under real operating constraints. Fraud detection, support operations, personalization, dispute handling, and engineering productivity all offer opportunities, but only when models are tied to good data and clear business metrics.

This chapter explains where AI and machine learning are genuinely useful in payment systems and where they are often oversold. The focus is on practical adoption: choosing use cases, understanding model risk, and integrating decision systems into regulated workflows.

---

## 33.1 AI in Payments: Practical View

AI/ML in payments is most effective when focused on measurable outcomes:

- lower fraud losses,
- better approval rates,
- improved customer experience,
- faster operations.

---

## 33.2 Fraud and Risk Models

Typical model applications:

- transaction risk scoring,
- anomaly detection,
- account takeover indicators,
- adaptive authentication recommendations.

Model quality depends on feature quality and feedback loops.

---

## 33.3 Personalization and Growth

AI can support:

- intelligent offers/rewards,
- better notification timing,
- churn risk prediction,
- customer support routing.

Personalization must respect consent and privacy rules.

---

## 33.4 Operational Optimization

AI/ML helps optimize:

- dispute triage,
- reconciliation exception prioritization,
- incident signal correlation,
- support workload forecasting.

---

## 33.5 NLP and Multimodal Opportunities

Transformer-based NLP can assist in:

- document understanding,
- policy search and summaries,
- support copilot workflows,
- compliance review acceleration.

Use human review for high-stakes decisions.

---

## 33.6 Model Governance Essentials

1. Data lineage and quality controls.
2. Bias/fairness monitoring where relevant.
3. Drift detection and retraining strategy.
4. Explainability and audit evidence.

---

## 33.7 Common Mistakes

1. Building models without production feedback loops.
2. No rollback strategy for degraded model performance.
3. Treating model output as unquestionable truth.

---

## 33.8 Glossary

- **Feature:** Input variable used by ML model.
- **Drift:** Change in data behavior that degrades model performance.
- **Explainability:** Ability to understand why model produced output.
- **NLP:** Natural Language Processing.

## 33.10 Recap

- AI/ML adds high value when tied to clear payment outcomes.
- Governance and monitoring are required for safe deployment.
- Human-in-the-loop remains important for critical workflows.
