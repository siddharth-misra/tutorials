# 35: Competitive Landscape

The payments market is crowded because different players attack different layers of the stack. Global networks, processors, fintech platforms, banks, wallet providers, and account-to-account rails each compete on a mix of acceptance, cost, speed, developer experience, and distribution.

This chapter explains competition as a layered systems problem rather than a winner-takes-all story. It helps you compare business models clearly and understand why companies that appear similar to customers may have very different sources of leverage.

---

## 35.1 Why Competition Is Multi-Layered

Competition happens at different layers:

- network rails,
- issuer/acquirer processing,
- consumer interface,
- merchant integration,
- value-added services.

No single company dominates every layer equally.

---

## 35.2 Major Competitive Groups

1. Global networks (for example Visa, Mastercard).
2. Closed-loop/alternative models (for example Amex in selected contexts).
3. Digital wallets/platform companies.
4. Processor and banking-platform providers.
5. Fintech challengers focused on niche experiences.

---

## 35.3 Strategic Differentiation Themes

- global acceptance and trust,
- reliability and security track record,
- partner ecosystem depth,
- speed of product innovation,
- data and analytics leverage.

---

## 35.4 Moats and Network Effects

Network effects can create strong defensibility: more participants increase value for everyone in the ecosystem.

But moats can erode without continuous innovation and operational excellence.

---

## 35.5 Where New Entrants Win

Challengers often win by:

- better user experience,
- lower integration friction,
- vertical-specific solutions,
- modern API-first distribution.

Incumbents often respond through partnerships and platform expansion.

---

## 35.6 Common Mistakes

1. Viewing competitors only as similar card networks.
2. Ignoring ecosystem economics beyond transaction fees.
3. Underestimating distribution and partner channels.

---

## 35.7 Glossary

- **Closed-loop network:** Same company controls both issuing and acquiring sides in a model.
- **Open-loop network:** Network connects many independent issuers and acquirers.
- **Moat:** Durable competitive advantage.
